Booking Help

Making a reservation on FastHotels is simple:

  1. Use the search form on our homepage to enter your destination, dates, and number of guests
  2. Browse the available hotels and select the one you prefer
  3. Choose your room type and review the booking details
  4. Enter your personal and payment information
  5. Confirm your booking

You'll receive a confirmation email with all the details of your reservation.

To modify or cancel your reservation:

  1. Log in to your FastHotels account
  2. Go to "My Bookings" in your profile
  3. Find the booking you want to modify or cancel
  4. Click on "Modify" or "Cancel" button
  5. Follow the instructions to complete the process

Please note that modification and cancellation policies vary by hotel and rate type. Some bookings may be non-refundable or subject to cancellation fees.

If you haven't received your confirmation email:

  1. Check your spam or junk folder
  2. Verify that you entered the correct email address during booking
  3. Log in to your account to check if the booking appears in "My Bookings"

If you still can't find your confirmation, please contact our customer support team for assistance.

Payment Issues

FastHotels accepts the following payment methods:

  • Credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express)
  • Debit cards
  • PayPal

The available payment methods may vary depending on the hotel and country.

The charging policy depends on the hotel and rate type you choose:

  • Pay now rates: Your card will be charged immediately at the time of booking
  • Pay later rates: Your card will be used to guarantee the reservation, but you'll pay directly at the hotel during your stay
  • Deposit rates: A partial payment will be charged at the time of booking, with the remaining balance due at the hotel

The payment policy is always clearly displayed during the booking process.

If you cancel a refundable booking:

  1. The refund will be processed back to your original payment method
  2. Processing time typically takes 5-10 business days, depending on your bank or credit card company
  3. You'll receive an email confirmation when the refund is processed

Please note that some bookings may be non-refundable or subject to partial refunds depending on the cancellation policy.

Account Support

To create a FastHotels account:

  1. Click on the "Sign Up" button in the top right corner of the website
  2. Enter your email address and create a password
  3. Fill in your personal information
  4. Verify your email address by clicking the link sent to your inbox

Once your account is created, you can manage your bookings, save favorite hotels, and enjoy a faster checkout process.

If you've forgotten your password:

  1. Click on "Login" in the top right corner
  2. Select "Forgot Password"
  3. Enter the email address associated with your account
  4. Check your email for a password reset link
  5. Follow the link to create a new password

For security reasons, password reset links expire after 24 hours.

To update your profile information:

  1. Log in to your FastHotels account
  2. Click on your name in the top right corner
  3. Select "Profile" from the dropdown menu
  4. Click "Edit Profile" to update your personal information
  5. Save your changes

You can update your name, contact information, address, and communication preferences.
